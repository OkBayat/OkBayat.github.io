# Photora
{: .no_toc }

{ Active startup · Product Photography · AI | fs-6 }

Photora is my AI-assisted product-photography startup. It is designed for businesses that want to turn ordinary product photos into polished advertising and social-media images without building a traditional photography workflow for every image.

I founded Photora in September 2026 and lead its product direction, software development, and technical architecture.

## Product Direction

The product is built around a simple workflow: a user uploads a product photo, chooses the intended format or dimensions, can add optional copy, and receives a generated output in a private dashboard.

The broader product direction includes use cases such as placing clothing on a mannequin, arranging fabric or products into styled scenes, and preparing images for social media, websites, catalogues, and advertising.

## My Role

**Founder and Software Engineer · September 2026 - Present**

I lead Photora's product direction and software development, including the web application, backend services, image-generation workflow, storage, testing, deployment, and the rules that keep generated images aligned with the source product.

Photora sits alongside [K2Quant](/work/projects/k2quant) and [Vocora](/work/projects/vocora) as a separate business with its own product and operating model.

## Current Status and Boundaries

Photora is an active startup under development. The current system is designed as a private web application with account-based uploads, image generation, output history, and usage limits.

The project uses artificial intelligence as part of the image-production workflow, but generated outputs still require appropriate product and visual review. This page describes the product direction and work that exists; it does not treat planned features, future distribution channels, or unverified outcomes as completed results.
